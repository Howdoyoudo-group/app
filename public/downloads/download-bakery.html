5 Trends to Watch
01
Free-from goes mainstream. Gluten-free, low-sugar and vegan bakes now standard range in all major retailers.
02
Premium indulgence. Consumers want treats that feel special - hybrid formats (cruffins, maritozzi) exploding.
03
Health-protein crossover. High-protein bread and snack bars bridging fitness and everyday bakery.
04
Social media formats. TikTok-viral products (crookie, filled croissants) driving NPD cycles.
05
HFSS regulation. From Oct 2025, sweet baked goods banned from volume promotions - reshaping trade.
