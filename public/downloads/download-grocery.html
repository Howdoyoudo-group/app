5 Trends to Watch
01
Discounter dominance. Aldi + Lidl at ~19% combined. Both opening 40–50 stores/year with no sign of stopping.
02
Private label surge. 50% of shoppers buying more own-label than ever. Retailers building premium house brands.
03
Online acceleration. Delivery up 11% YoY. Ocado at record share. Q-commerce settling into top-up missions.
04
Loyalty as moat. Clubcard and Nectar locking in households. Shoppers visiting fewer stores than at any time since 2021.
05
AI & tech in store. Computer vision checkouts, AI stock management and hyper-personalised promotions going mainstream.
