5 Trends to Watch
01
Sustainable travel mandate. Consumers demanding eco-credentials. Airlines and OTAs investing in carbon offsetting and green options.
02
Wellness tourism boom. Spa, yoga, and mindfulness retreats growing fastest. Bath, Cotswolds key UK destinations.
03
Experiential over transactional. Heritage tours, culinary tourism, adventure - unique experiences beating standard sun/sea.
04
Cruise comeback. Cruise fastest-growing segment. UK is #2 in Europe. New ships and itineraries driving demand.
05
AI travel planning. AI travel agents, chatbot bookings and personalised itinerary tools reshaping the booking journey.
