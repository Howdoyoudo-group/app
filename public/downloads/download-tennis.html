5 Trends to Watch
1
Padel explosion — UK padel courts grew 400% (2021–24). LTA investing heavily; creates new coaching and venue roles.
2
Amazon Prime reshaping broadcast — ATP rights deal (2023–28) moves tennis off free-to-air. New digital production roles emerging.
3
Electronic line calling — Hawk-Eye replacing line judges at all Wimbledon courts. Tech and officiating roles evolving.
4
LTA £1bn facility investment — Park courts, indoor centres, schools programmes. Coaching and development jobs growing fast.
5
Player data economy — IBM, SAP and Infosys partnerships at Slams create demand for sports analytics and data engineering roles.
