5 Trends to Watch
01
Hybrid work & home office. Remote working sustained demand for home office furniture and dedicated workspace design.
02
Sustainability imperative. Recycled materials, eco-certifications and circular design growing at 7%+ CAGR.
03
Online AR shopping. Wayfair, IKEA AR tools reducing returns - customers visualising before buying. Online = 5.82% CAGR.
04
Instagram-driven trends. Warmer tones, maximalism, and 'quiet luxury' aesthetic driven by social media content cycles.
05
Independent revival. Spending at independent homeware stores up 29% 2023–25. Big-box retailers losing share to niche specialists.
