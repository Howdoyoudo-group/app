5 Trends to Watch
1
Off-stage skills shortage - 98% of theatres report at least one hard-to-fill technical vacancy. Sound, lighting, automation and wigs/hair/makeup roles are hit hardest (SOLT/UK Theatre/Gatsby, 2025).
2
Exodus of crew to screen - film and TV production is drawing backstage talent away from theatre with better pay and conditions, an ongoing concern reported by The Stage.
3
West End at a record high - 2025 admissions (17.6m) and box office revenue (£1bn+) both hit all-time highs, outpacing pre-pandemic levels and beating Premier League and Broadway attendance.
4
Rising costs squeeze subsidised venues - energy bills up 120% and National Insurance changes are pressuring theatres even as audiences grow; the National Theatre's own production income fell 17% in 2024/25.
5
New funding round ahead - Arts Council England's next National Portfolio Organisation cycle (2028-33) opens for applications in October 2026, the biggest funding decision UK theatre faces this parliament.
