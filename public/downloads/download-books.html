5 Trends to Watch
01
BookTok drives physical sales. A viral BookTok moment can lift a title's sales up to 9x - accounting for up to 30% of sales in romance, YA and fantasy.
02
Digital audiobooks are the fastest-growing format. UK digital audiobook revenue grew 10% in 2025, now 10% of the whole consumer publishing market.
03
Exports now the majority of UK revenue. 64% of the UK's record £7.4bn publishing income in 2025 came from overseas sales.
04
Self-publishing keeps growing. Amazon KDP and platforms like Reedsy are lowering the barrier to entry, blurring the line between traditional and independent publishing.
05
AI and copyright. Publishers and authors are pushing back on AI training-data use, with licensing deals and copyright protection now a major industry battleground.
