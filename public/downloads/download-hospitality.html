5 Trends to Watch
01
Plant-based maturation. After hype peak, plant-based stabilising - quality and taste parity with meat now key battleground.
02
Low/no alcohol. Guinness 0.0, alcohol-free spirits, functional mocktails all booming. Gen Z most sober generation.
03
Private label surge. Own-label at record penetration - even in premium categories. Discounters driving quality standards up.
04
Food delivery plateau. Deliveroo and Uber Eats growth slowing as consumers cook at home more. Profitability focus sharpens.
05
Regenerative food. Supply chain transparency, carbon labelling, and regenerative agriculture moving from niche to mainstream.
