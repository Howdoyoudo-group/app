5 Trends to Watch
01
NHS crisis = private opportunity. Record NHS waiting lists driving patients to self-pay and insurance-funded private care.
02
Telehealth physio. Post-COVID, virtual consultations and digital exercise platforms (Kaia, Hinge Health) disrupting model.
03
Workplace wellbeing demand. Employers investing in physio-as-benefit. Remote work MSK surge creating corporate opportunity.
04
Women's health surge. Pelvic floor, menopause, maternal physio becoming mainstream - significant under-served market.
05
AI diagnostics. Movement analysis AI and wearables enabling remote assessment and personalised rehab programmes.
