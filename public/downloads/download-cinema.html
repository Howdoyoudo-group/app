5 Trends to Watch
01
Inward investment boom. US studios and streamers account for 85% of UK production spend. UK = #1 global hub outside US.
02
Streaming bifurcation. Netflix (17.6m) and Amazon (13.6m) dominate; smaller SVODs struggling with churn.
03
Indie sector under threat. 70%+ of indie producers at risk. Finance access described as acute crisis by BFI.
04
AI in production. AI being used for script development, VFX, casting and scheduling - industry-wide anxiety and opportunity.
05
Harry Potter effect. HBO reboot, Avengers: Doomsday, and major HETV commissions securing UK studio space through 2030.
