5 Trends to Watch
01
Shein disruption. Chinese ultra-fast fashion growing 32% annually - reshaping price expectations across market.
02
Resale as default. Vinted and eBay top-3 UK fashion destinations. Resale now a £3bn+ market, not fringe.
03
Brand sustainability mandates. 73% of fashion brands investing in circular economy. Transparency & traceability demanded.
04
Social commerce explosion. 42.4m TikTok purchases in UK 2024. Live shopping events and creator drops reshaping launch strategy.
05
Physical retail revival. Experiential flagship stores (Primark, Zara) driving footfall. Physical not dead - it's differentiating.
