5 Trends to Watch
01
Mobile dominates user base. 59% of UK gamers use mobile. Children's and hyper-casual driving new user acquisition.
02
Subscription gaming. Xbox Game Pass and PS Plus reshaping how games are monetised and discovered.
03
Esports mainstream. University leagues, brand sponsorships, and stadium events cementing esports as entertainment.
04
Chinese platform pressure. Tencent-backed titles and mobile platforms gaining share. Regulatory scrutiny increasing.
05
AI in game development. AI tools slashing development costs, enabling indie studios and accelerating AAA production cycles.
