5 Trends to Watch
01
EV transition pressure. ZEV mandate forcing manufacturers to subsidise EVs by £11k each - unsustainable long term.
02
Chinese invasion. Omoda and Jaecoo (Chery) entering top-10; MG volumes strong. BYD expanding.
03
Fleet carries market. Private buyers still cautious - 61% of new sales to fleet/business, not consumers.
04
SUV dominance. Ford Puma, Kia Sportage top models. SUV and crossover body styles define volume market.
05
Software-defined cars. OTA updates, subscriptions and in-car services becoming key revenue and loyalty battleground.
