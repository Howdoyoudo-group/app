5 Trends to Watch
01
Longevity science mainstream. Biohacking, NAD+ supplements, CGM devices - science-backed longevity entering mass market.
02
Gut health as foundation. Microbiome research, probiotics, fermented foods - gut health becoming primary wellness driver.
03
Corporate wellness mandated. Post-COVID, employers investing in workplace wellbeing. EAP, gym subsidies, mental health days.
04
Wearables intelligence. Apple Watch, Whoop, Oura Ring - continuous health monitoring now mainstream for ~15% of UK adults.
05
Wellness tourism premium. Bath, Cotswolds, Scotland destinations growing. Thermal spas, retreat weekends at premium.
