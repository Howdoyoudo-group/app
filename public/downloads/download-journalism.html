5 Trends to Watch
01
Subscription or die. Paywall models now essential. The Times, FT, Guardian, Economist all growing digital subs.
02
Local news collapse. 200+ local titles closed since 2005. Government-backed Local Democracy Reporters a sticking plaster.
03
AI threat and opportunity. AI generating content at scale - NUJ and publishers negotiating licensing; deepfakes undermining trust.
04
Creator / newsletter rise. Substack, Ghost enabling journalists to build independent audiences and subscription revenue.
05
Trust crisis. UK public trust in news at record lows. Polarisation and misinformation challenging journalism's role.
