5 Trends to Watch
01
Lab-grown diamonds boom. Pandora switched to lab-grown diamonds; UK consumers embracing ethical, affordable alternatives.
02
Personalisation demand. Name necklaces, birthstone pieces, engraving - bespoke-feel at mass-market price.
03
Accessible luxury growth. Monica Vinader, Missoma bridging gap between costume and fine jewellery. Millennials leading.
04
Resale and authentication. Christie's, Sotheby's growing secondary market. Blockchain authentication emerging.
05
Men's jewellery rising. Signet rings, chains, and minimal bands driving meaningful male jewellery category growth.
