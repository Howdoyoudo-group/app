5 Trends to Watch
01
Trainers as default. Sneakers dominate all age groups, blurring athletic and lifestyle.
02
Sustainability pressure. 47%+ of consumers globally prefer sustainable options; compostable materials gaining ground.
03
DTC & social commerce. TikTok & Instagram driving impulse; Nike and Adidas cutting wholesale.
04
Resale boom. Vinted & eBay now top-3 UK channels for second-hand footwear.
05
Made in UK moment. Post-Liberation Day tariffs creating opportunity for British heritage brands.
