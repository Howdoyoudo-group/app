5 Trends to Watch
01
Portal dominance. Rightmove and Zoopla capture 90%+ of buyer search. Portal fees are existential cost for agents.
02
Mortgage rate stabilisation. Rates stabilising at 3-year low driving buyer return. Demand above supply in most markets.
03
Hybrid model growth. Purplebricks, Strike, eXp disrupting on price. Traditional agents fighting back with service.
04
Rental crisis. Landlord exodus reducing supply; rents at record highs. CMA investigating letting agent fees.
05
AI valuation & search. PropTech platforms using AI for automated valuations, smart matching and digital viewings.
