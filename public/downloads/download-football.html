5 Trends to Watch
01
International rights boom. For first time, international broadcast revenue (£6.5bn) exceeds domestic (£6.7bn) over deal cycle.
02
Saudi / private equity. Saudi PIF, US PE funds reshaping ownership and inflating wage bills. Financial sustainability concerns.
03
Independent Regulator. New IFR legislation creating first independent football regulator - major structural shift for English game.
04
Women's football growth. WSL £65m broadcast deal with Sky/BBC from 2025/26. Attendance and sponsorship records broken.
05
Fan engagement & data. Clubs monetising direct-to-consumer, data analytics, and stadium hospitality to diversify revenue.
