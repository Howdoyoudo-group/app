5 Trends to Watch
01
Price war intensifying. McDonald's, Greggs and Wetherspoon's value coffee squeezing mid-market chains on price.
02
Cold brew & iced dominates. Gen Z prefers cold formats; iced coffee now year-round, not seasonal.
03
Premium home brewing. Pod sales projected £899m (2025) to £1.15bn (2030). Nespresso, Sage, De'Longhi growing.
04
Drive-thru expansion. 855 UK drive-thru coffee sites. Costa and Starbucks both investing heavily in format.
05
Franchise momentum. Net new outlets up +2.4% in 2025. Franchise model enabling rapid regional expansion for challenger brands.
