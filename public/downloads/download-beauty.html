5 Trends to Watch
01
Skincare-first culture. Skincare overtaking colour as lead category; Gen Z spending more on serums than foundation.
02
K-Beauty dominance. Korean brands (COSRX, Laneige) selling at record pace in Boots and independents.
03
Clean beauty mandate. Vegan, cruelty-free, sustainable packaging now table stakes, not premium differentiator.
04
Male grooming boom. Men's skincare growing fastest - luxury brands like Dior and Chanel targeting young men.
05
TikTok commerce. Social commerce = 23% of UK online fashion/beauty purchases. Influencer-led launches dominate NPD.
