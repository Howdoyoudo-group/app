5 Trends to Watch
01
Green skills boom. Heat pump installers, solar PV fitters and EV charger engineers are the fastest-growing trades in the UK. The 2035 boiler ban is accelerating demand.
02
Trade shortage crisis. 50,000+ electrician and plumber shortfall projected by 2028. Apprenticeship completions down. Wages rising sharply as a result.
03
Platform disruption. Checkatrade, MyBuilder and TaskRabbit are shifting customer acquisition online — tradespeople with strong reviews earn 30% more than peers.
04
Retrofit wave. Government's Warm Homes Plan targets 5m homes insulated or upgraded by 2030, creating a sustained demand surge for cavity wall, loft and EWI trades.
05
Trade business ownership. Self-employed tradespeople increasingly running multi-person operations. Business skills (quoting, invoicing, CRM) becoming as important as craft.
