5 Trends to Watch
01
Oasis reunion economy. 1.4m fans and back-catalogue surge - proving heritage acts' commercial power in streaming era.
02
Vinyl revival real. 18th consecutive year of growth. Physical music now growing - cultural signalling and collector market.
03
Ticket price backlash. Dynamic pricing (Oasis controversy) driving consumer and regulatory pressure on Ticketmaster.
04
AI and artist rights. AI-generated music flooding platforms; labels and artists battling for licensing and copyright protection.
05
Global reach of UK talent. Olivia Dean, Sam Fender, Sleep Token - UK artists competing globally. BPI pushing international growth.
