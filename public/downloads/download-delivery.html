5 Trends to Watch
01
EV fleet transition. DPD, Royal Mail and Amazon are mass-electrifying last-mile fleets. 700,000 electric delivery vehicles expected on UK roads by 2030.
02
Same-day becomes standard. Amazon, Ocado and quick-commerce startups have reset consumer expectations. 2-hour and same-day delivery now table stakes in urban areas.
03
Driver classification battles. Gig economy legal cases (Uber, Deliveroo) reshaping contractor rights. Minimum pay guarantees now standard for many platforms.
04
Automation in warehousing. Ocado's robot warehouses, Amazon's robotic fulfilment centres and dark stores are reducing headcount — but creating new tech roles.
05
Returns economy. UK consumers return 25–30% of online fashion orders. Returns processing is now a £3bn+ sub-sector with specialist operators like ZigZag Global.
