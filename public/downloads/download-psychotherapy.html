5 Trends to Watch
01
Mental health destigmatisation. Gen Z most likely to seek therapy. Mental health normalised as health maintenance, not crisis response.
02
Digital therapy boom. Apps (Headspace, Calm, BetterHelp) and digital CBT platforms growing rapidly. Ieso, Spill, Oliva scaling.
03
Workplace demand. Post-COVID, employer-funded EAP and workplace therapy platforms growing fastest segment.
04
NHS crisis = private growth. 18-week+ waits pushing hundreds of thousands into self-funded private therapy annually.
05
Regulation debate. Psychotherapy/counselling unregulated in UK - professional bodies pushing for statutory regulation.
