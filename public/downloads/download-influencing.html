5 Trends to Watch
01
From influencers to creators. Brands shifting from one-off posts to long-term creator partnerships and "always-on" content programmes.
02
Long-tail wins. Mid-tier and niche creators (10k–500k followers) outperform megastars on engagement and ROI - agencies restructuring around them.
03
AI-augmented creation. Editing, thumbnails, scripting and dubbing collapse from days to minutes - solo creators now run media businesses.
04
Creator-led brands. Logan Paul (Prime), MrBeast (Feastables), Emma Chamberlain (Chamberlain Coffee) - content as the cheapest customer-acquisition channel.
05
UK regulation tightens. ASA & CMA cracking down on undisclosed ads. Agencies investing in compliance, contracts and image-rights expertise.
