5 Trends to Watch
01
Housebuilding pressure. Government's 1.5m homes target is reshaping planning rules, land release and developer incentives — creating a sustained pipeline.
02
Skills crisis deepens. Average age of a UK tradesperson is 45+. An estimated 40,000 workers leave the sector annually. Apprenticeship take-up must double.
03
Modern Methods of Construction. Offsite manufacturing, modular homes and pre-assembled units cutting build times and waste. Slowly entering mainstream.
04
Net zero retrofit. 29 million UK homes need energy upgrades by 2050. Heat pumps, insulation and EV charging points driving a huge retrofit market.
05
Digital & BIM adoption. Building Information Modelling now standard on public contracts. Drone surveys, AI-driven scheduling and digital twins reshaping site operations.
