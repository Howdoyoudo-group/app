5 Trends to Watch
01
Demand-income squeeze. Rising cost-of-living both increases need for services and reduces donations simultaneously.
02
NIC cost shock. April 2025 employer NI rise costing sector hundreds of millions - many reducing headcount.
03
Digital fundraising. Online and recurring giving growing. Platforms like JustGiving and GoFundMe reshaping fundraising.
04
Merger pressure. Small charities merging to survive; consolidation accelerating in crowded sub-sectors.
05
Impact measurement. Funders increasingly demanding robust evidence of outcomes, raising reporting costs for smaller orgs.
