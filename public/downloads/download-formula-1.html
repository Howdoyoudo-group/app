5 Trends to Watch
01
2026 regulation reset. New aero rules, active aero and fully electric energy recovery. Teams are redesigning cars from scratch - huge recruitment wave.
02
Cadillac / GM entry. 11th team on the grid from 2026, building a UK factory at Silverstone. First new constructor entry since 2016.
03
Cost cap reshaping teams. $135m cap forcing efficiency, smaller squads, and smarter hiring. Commercial and data roles growing fastest.
04
US market explosion. Three US races (Miami, Austin, Las Vegas). F1's Netflix-driven fanbase driving sponsorship and media investment.
05
Sustainability mandates. 100% sustainable fuels from 2026. Net-zero targets driving new engineering and ESG roles across all teams.
