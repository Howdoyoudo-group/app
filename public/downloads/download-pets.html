5 Trends to Watch
01
Humanisation of pets. Pets as family - driving premium food, health supplements, insurance, and specialist services.
02
Vet market scrutiny. CMA investigating £2bn veterinary market. Proposing price caps, banning prescribing bonuses.
03
Cultivated meat. Meatly's lab-grown chicken dog treats launched at Pets at Home Feb 2025 - world first.
04
DTC subscription boom. Butternut Box, Tails.com disrupting traditional pet food retail with personalised delivery.
05
Gen Z pet parents. Under-25s spending most per month on pets - delaying children, investing in pet wellbeing.
